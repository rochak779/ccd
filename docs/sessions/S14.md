## Outcome

PDF, CSV and TXT now use the shared parsed-source contract introduced for workbooks. PDF evidence retains real page boundaries and exact excerpts; CSV evidence resolves source rows by header; TXT evidence resolves inclusive line ranges. Batch parsing retains successful sources while reporting password-protected, OCR-required, unsupported and parse-failed inputs independently. Cached extraction is accepted only when its hash and extracted content match the source bytes.
